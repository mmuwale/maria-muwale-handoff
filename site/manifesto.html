“I believe our academic experience should be just as engaging, connected and rewarding as our social life at Strathmore. As a class representative in both my previous and current years, and through my service in the 17th Academic Subcommittee, I have gained firsthand experience listening to students, working with representatives and understanding how student leadership works. I bring initiative, teamwork and a practical approach to leadership, and I am ready to turn ideas into meaningful action for students.”

THE 4 PILLARS

Smart Learning Spaces

A proposed feature integrated into the MyStrath app, that allows students to see available classrooms, labs and lecture theatres, and navigate to them, with the wider system exploring other student services that could be digitised.

Making it easier for students to discover suitable learning spaces and find their way around campus - by exploring a practical, university-approved pathway for better visibility of available spaces through existing systems.

Interfaculty Projects

Creating more meaningful academic challenges and collaborative projects that bring students across different courses and faculties together to solve real-world problems and learn from different principles.

Starting with manageable pilots that bring students from different disciplines together to tackle real-world challenges and build solutions that no single field could create alone.

Industrial Visits

Working with lecturers and relevant partners to expand educational visits that connect students with workplaces, industries and career opportunities beyond the classroom.

Working with lecturers and relevant stakeholders to identify gaps, coordinate student demand and support meaningful exposure to workplaces and industries beyond the classroom.

Faculty Spotlight Week

A week celebrating every faculty by showcasing its programmes, projects, opportunities, student life, achievements and the people behind them.

An academic discovery and collaboration week where students can discover projects, research, opportunities and expertise beyond their own timetables - and see how different disciplines connect.

Each pillar begins with listening and one manageable pilot, not a university-wide launch. By the end of October 2026 all four have a first documented step; a feasibility decision on Smart Learning Spaces by the end of January 2027; the first interfaculty challenge and the first Faculty Spotlight Week by the end of April 2027. After each pilot or event, a short outcome summary is published.

These are proposed milestones, not promises that depend on approvals outside the Female academic Rep’s control, and they assume a September 2026 start to the term. Not every idea can be delivered directly by one representative. The role is to represent, coordinate, advocate, follow up and help turn viable student priorities into action.
